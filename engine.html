COMPLETE 3D ENGINE ASSEMBLY
INDEPENDENT MODELING WORK

From 2D drawings to 3D assembly

Starting with provided engineering drawings, I recreated the engine's individual components in Siemens NX, including the finned cylinder, flywheel, linkage, support geometry, and base. I then positioned the components into a complete assembly, translating drawing information into detailed 3D geometry.

The original engine design and reference drawings were supplied; my individual contribution was the part modeling and assembly work.
